## House Sales in King County, USA

[House_Sales_in_King_Count_USA_Data Analysis with Python_IBM.ipynb](House_Sales_in_King_Count_USA_Data%20Analysis%20with%20Python_IBM.ipynb)

Course project on house sales from May 2014 to May 2015 in King County (Seattle area): data wrangling (mean imputation for bedrooms and bathrooms), exploratory analysis, and model development. Results in the notebook: R² of about 0.49 for a single-feature linear model, about 0.66 with multiple features, about 0.75 with a polynomial pipeline, and about 0.70 on the test set with a polynomial ridge regression.
