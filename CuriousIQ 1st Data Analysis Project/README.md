# AdventureWorks Sales Analysis — CuriousIQ Data Analysis Project 1

**Course:** CuriousIQ Diploma in Data Analysis · **Date:** April 2022 · **Type:** individual business case project

## Business brief

A Business Intelligence Manager at the fictional company AdventureWorks asked for insights for a CEO presentation ([brief](Data%20Analysis%20Project.pdf)):

- **Product association:** which products are usually sold together?
- **Geography:** should sales effort focus on a specific area?
- **Dead products:** which products rarely sell and could be discontinued?
- **Customers:** who are the best customers, and how should they be rewarded?

## What I did

1. Queried the AdventureWorks (`SalesLT`) database in SQL Server: a self-join on order lines to find product pairs bought in the same order, revenue by country/state/city, products with no or few sales, and revenue per customer ([Project.sql](Project.sql)).
2. Built pivot tables and charts in Excel: product associations, geography, dead products, customers, and a dashboard sheet ([Project.xlsx](Project.xlsx)).
3. Presented the findings and recommendations in a 9-slide deck ([Project.pptx](Project.pptx)).

## Findings presented

- Most frequently bought products were accessories: Sport-100 helmets, long-sleeve logo jerseys, the AWC logo cap.
- Revenue split between two regions was about 60% / 40%, used to argue where to focus sales effort.
- Many road and mountain frame models rarely sold, making them candidates for review.
- The top five customers each accounted for roughly 9–13% of revenue, the basis for a rewards proposal.
